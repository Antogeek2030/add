Why Fall Gutter Cleaning Matters More Than You Think

🍂

As the leaves begin to fall across Woodsbridge, many homeowners put gutter cleaning on the “I’ll get to it later” list. Unfortunately, “later” often becomes winter — and by then the damage may already be underway.

What Happens When Gutters Stay Clogged

Clogged gutters force water to overflow and run down the exterior walls or pool near the foundation. Over time this can lead to:

  • Basement or crawl-space seepage
  • Siding damage and staining
  • Fascia and soffit rot
  • Ice dams in freezing weather
  • Landscape erosion near the house

Beyond Leaves: The Hidden Debris

It’s not just leaves. Shingle grit, twigs, pine needles, and even small animal nests accumulate throughout the year. A single heavy rain after a dry spell can overwhelm a partially blocked system and send water where it doesn’t belong.

When to Schedule Cleaning

In most of our service area, the ideal window is late October through mid-November — after the majority of leaves have fallen but before consistent freezing temperatures arrive. Homes under heavy tree cover may need a second cleaning in spring.

Professional vs. DIY

While some homeowners are comfortable on a ladder, gutter cleaning involves working at height with tools and debris. A professional crew can complete the job safely, inspect the full system (hangers, seams, downspouts), and spot small problems before they become expensive repairs.
